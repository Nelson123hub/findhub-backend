About Federal University of Technology, Minna

The Federal University of Technology, Minna (FUTMINNA) is a top-tier institution located in Minna, Niger State, Nigeria. Established in 1983, the university is committed to training skilled and innovative workforce that would transform Nigeria's natural resources into goods and services.

With over 20,000 students across multiple campuses including the Main Campus (Gidan Kwano) and Bosso Campus, FUTMINNA offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and pre-degree programmes across 10 schools and over 50 departments.

Our Lost & Found platform is designed to serve the entire FUTMINNA community, helping students and staff reconnect with their lost belongings through a secure, efficient, and transparent system.

Platform Features

Everything you need to report, find, and recover lost items on campus

Image Upload

Upload clear images of lost or found items to help with identification and matching. Visual evidence significantly increases recovery chances.

Real-time Notifications

Get instant alerts when a potential match is found for your lost item. Never miss an opportunity to recover your belongings.

Smart Search & Matching

Advanced search and matching algorithms help connect lost items with their rightful owners based on descriptions, locations, and categories.

Status Tracking

Track your item through every stage: Reported → Under Review → Matched → Claim Initiated → Verified → Returned → Closed.

Secure Verification

Security questions and image comparison ensure items are returned to their true owners. Admin verification prevents fraudulent claims.

Admin Dashboard

Administrators can manage all reports, verify claims, generate statistics, and configure system settings for optimal operation.

How It Works
